Welcome to WISDOM English School

Located in Gangaur, Madhubani district of Bihar, WISDOM English School is committed to providing quality English-medium education in a safe, disciplined and joyful environment.

  • Strong English-medium curriculum
  • Modern Computer Laboratory
  • Experienced & caring teachers
  • Co-curricular activities & celebrations
  • Focus on overall personality development

We believe every child has unique potential. Our goal is to help students become confident, responsible and successful individuals.
